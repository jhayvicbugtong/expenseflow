FAQ

Questions

How much does ExpenseFlow cost?

₱49, paid once when you download it from the App Store or Google Play. There’s no monthly subscription and no ads, and every feature is included.

How accurate is the receipt scanner?

It reads the merchant, date, and total in under 3 seconds, and you always see the result before it’s saved, so you can fix anything it got wrong. You can also add transactions by hand.

Do I need to connect my bank or e-wallet?

No. You create your wallets yourself, which also covers cash spending that e-wallets don’t see. Automatic import from GCash and Maya is planned for Q3 2027 and will be optional.

Is my data safe?

Receipt images are encrypted and never shared with third parties, and we never ask for bank passwords. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).

Can I use it for my BIR records?

ExpenseFlow keeps your receipt photos and categorised expenses together and exports them to CSV, which makes record-keeping easier for freelancers and small businesses. It doesn’t file taxes for you.

When can I download it?
