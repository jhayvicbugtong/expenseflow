About us

Made by students who live on a budget too

ExpenseFlow started as our Technopreneurship project. We asked students and young workers how they handle their money, and the answer was the same everywhere: they spend every day but don’t track it. Our mission is to help every young Filipino understand where their money goes, in a way that’s simple, free, and made for pesos.

FAQ

Questions

Is ExpenseFlow really free?

Yes. Recording, the Financial Calendar, budgets, bill reminders, and reports are free with no ads. Premium is optional at ₱49 a month.

Do I need to connect my bank or e-wallet?

No. You add expenses yourself, which also covers cash spending like fare, food, and sari-sari store purchases that e-wallets don’t see.

Is my data safe?

Your records are stored on your phone and can be locked with your fingerprint or face. We don’t ask for bank passwords.

When can I download it?

ExpenseFlow is in development. We are testing our prototype now and plan to launch on Android first.
